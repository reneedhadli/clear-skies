ENGL 102 — Introduction to Literary Analysis
University of Alberta · Fall Term · 3 credits
Instructor: Dr. Lena Moreau
Seminar: Tuesday and Thursday 12:30–1:50, Humanities 2-14

COURSE DESCRIPTION
A friendly introduction to reading closely and writing clearly about fiction, poetry,
and drama. Expect discussion, short responses, and three essays.

GRADE BREAKDOWN
Participation 10% · Close reading response 2% · Essays 26% · Peer review 2% ·
Presentation 5% · Final portfolio 20% · Final exam 35%

READING AND WRITING SCHEDULE
Week 1  Welcome, how to read a story
Week 2  Voice and point of view
Week 3  Close reading response due Tuesday, 2%
Week 4  Character and conflict
Week 5  Setting and atmosphere
Week 6  Essay 1: short fiction due Friday, 6%
Week 7  Peer review workshop on Thursday, 2%
Week 8  Reading week — read something for fun
Week 9  Essay 2: poetry due Friday, 10%
Week 10 Poetry presentations
Week 11 Class presentation on Tuesday, 5%
Week 12 Essay 3: drama due Friday, 10%
Week 13 Revision and portfolio workshop
Week 14 Final portfolio due Thursday, 20%
Week 15 Final exam (date set by registrar), 35%

COURSE POLICIES
Bring the assigned text to every seminar. Essays use MLA format. You may request a
48-hour extension once, no questions asked, if you email before the deadline.
