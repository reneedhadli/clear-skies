MATH 125 — Linear Algebra I
University of Alberta · Fall Term · 3 credits
Instructor: Dr. Hannah Okafor

COURSE DESCRIPTION
Vectors, systems of linear equations, matrices, determinants, eigenvalues, and
diagonalization. We focus on both computation and geometric intuition.
