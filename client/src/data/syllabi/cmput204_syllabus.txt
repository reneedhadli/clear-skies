CMPUT 204 — Algorithms I
University of Alberta · Fall Term · 3 credits
Instructor: Dr. Marcus Chen
Lectures: Monday, Wednesday, Friday 11:00–11:50, CCIS 1-140

COURSE DESCRIPTION
How do we know an algorithm is good? We will study asymptotic analysis, divide and
conquer, sorting, graph algorithms, greedy methods, and dynamic programming, with
proofs and hands-on problem sets.

ASSESSMENT
Quizzes 6% · Assignments 32% · Midterm 27% · Final exam 35%

WEEKLY PLAN
Week 1  Introduction and stable matching
Week 2  Asymptotic notation and runtime analysis
Week 3  Divide and conquer. Quiz 1: asymptotics on Wednesday, 3%
Week 4  Mergesort and recurrences. Assignment 1: divide & conquer due Friday, 8%
Week 5  Heaps and priority queues
Week 6  Graph search and BFS/DFS
Week 7  Midterm on Tuesday, 20%. In class, closed book.
Week 8  Reading week — rest and catch up
Week 9  Shortest paths. Assignment 2: graphs due Friday, 15%
Week 10 Minimum spanning trees
Week 11 Greedy algorithms. Assignment 3: greedy due Friday, 12%
Week 12 Dynamic programming
Week 13 Network flow and review
Week 14 Final exam on Thursday, 35%
Week 15 No classes
